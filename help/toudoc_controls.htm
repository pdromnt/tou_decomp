6.0 Controls & how to use them

This is very important to read!

When you're flying:

Use your thrust, back, turn left and turn right -keys to control your ship. Menu button will launch all your bullets that can be launched, if you are not on repair place (for example: Nuclear barrel power 2, both Nalles, Komet Bomb 3, Fireworks and so on. Read weapons for more information).

IMPORTANT: If you get stuck, hold down your turn left and turn right -keys (yes, both of them) for a second, and your ship will make an emergency teleport that drains some energy.

While at a repair place:

Use turn left and turn right -keys to change your special weapon. Press basic weapon-key to change your special weapon's power. Read weapons for more information on which weapons have differenct powers. Press menu button to enter weapons-menu. In weapons-menu, use thrust, back, turn left and turn right -keys to select your weapon. Press menu button again to exit weapons-menu.

When you're dead:

Use your thrust, back, turn left and turn right -keys to view the level. Press menu button to reborn, if you have lives left and if you have set Player must wait for reborn to "no" from Options / Rules.

 

Default control buttons:

  Thrust Back Turn left Turn right Basic weapon Special weapon Menu button
Player 1 Up arrow Down arrow Left arrow Right arrow Right shift Right ctrl Slash (next to right shift)
Player 2 W S A D Tab Q 1
Player 3 I K J L B V N
Player 4 Numpad 5 Numpad 2 Numpad 1 Numpad 3 Numpad 7 Numpad 8 Numpad 9

You can easily change these keys from Options / controls to anything you like
