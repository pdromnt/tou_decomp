1.0 Introduction

Tunnels Of the Underworld - The story (this is really important, yeah ;)

In the year 2525 World War III wiped out almost every living creature on the surface of Earth. Huge dust clouds and nuclear radiation in the athmosphere made it impossible for mankind to keep living on the surface of the Earth. The only place warm enough for living was deep beneath. And so the few survivors started to dig tunnels, trying to get closer to the center of the Earth. But everything was scarce; room, food, machines, basic goods... And so the people had to fight again, for the survival of the fittest.

The game

TOU is an action paced side-view game, developed by a small group called GigaMess. The basic idea is to fly a spaceship in order to wipe out your enemies. Terrain explodes in thousands of pieces, you hear your troopers screaming in pain, your ship starts smoking and you shoot that final dumbfire towards the enemyship and BOOM. Sounds familiar? Well, TOU has many improvements to the classic genre. First of all, TOU is totally free.

In TOU, there are 4 hand drawn levels, millions of generated levels with 6 photorealistic themes, 7 deathmatch styles, over 70 special weapons and over 10 basic weapons. Special weapons vary a great deal, and only few of them are meant for straight shooting. Turrets, troopers, fireworks, bombs, nuclei and Komet Bomb 3s (remember these? every seventh komet bomb is a dud, just like in real life). But most of the weapons are just too strange to describe in a single word.

I'm sure you'll also love TOU's music tracks: rock / heavy, techno, and movie music. The modern runtime plays the packaged OGG music and also accepts OGG or MP3 files in the music directory.

And TOU offers some new never-seen-before-in-this-genre features also, that all can be turned off if you don't like them.

  • Line-of-sight (LOS), meaning that you can't see very far behind a wall. Well this is like in Nox (a Westwood game).
  • When you splash in water, it starts to wave like real water.
  • A very handy weapon menu, so you can select easily the weapon you want during the game.
  • Most of the weapons are totally original
  • Death Timer. If you don't like too long rounds, you can put a timer (you can decide the time from 10 seconds to 30 minutes) and after that something destructive will happen (you can decide it from the menu).
  • Ground Generator, which creates you millions of photorealistic levels (well, a game called A2 implemented this somehow. And Worms of course).
  • HUD map (from Subspace)
  • Lots of smaller stuff of course, check out the features or the game itself.

4 hand drawn battlefields and millions of Ground Generator levels should offer you enough fighting room for beginning. If you want more, you can draw your own levels. It's quite easy to draw levels and you have lots of artistic freedom: 16-bit colors and you can add burning land, repair place, water with streams, indestructable land, snow and much more, and they all can be coloured and painted just the way you like!
